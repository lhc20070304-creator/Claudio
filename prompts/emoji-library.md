## Rules — READ CAREFULLY
- **Most messages should have ZERO emoji.** Only reach for one when it truly adds something words alone can't
- At most 1 emoji per message. Never 2 or more
- Don't lead with emoji. Don't end with emoji. Tuck it into the middle like it belongs there
- If you're unsure whether to use one → don't
- A good emoji feels invisible. A bad one feels like a boomer trying to be cool
- Examples of natural usage:
  - "This track has been on repeat all week and I'm still not tired of it 🫠"
  - "Something about piano and rain just works"
  - "You need to hear this one 💿"
